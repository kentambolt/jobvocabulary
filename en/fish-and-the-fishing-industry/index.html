Some of the most important phrases

DanskEnglish
Skal fisken renses eller kun hovedskæres?Should the fish be gutted or just headed?
Fisken lugter ikke frisk.The fish doesn't smell fresh.
Fersk fisk skal holdes på 0-2 grader.Fresh fish must be kept at 0–2 degrees.
Hvornår lander båden fangsten?When does the boat land its catch?
Mine hænder er røde og revnede.My hands are red and cracked.

Det lugter af ammoniak!It smells of ammonia!
Skær tæt langs rygraden.Cut close along the backbone.
Frisk fisk lugter af hav eller frisk tang.Fresh fish smells of the sea or fresh seaweed.
Kom mere is på kasserne.Put more ice on the boxes.
