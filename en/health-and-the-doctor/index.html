Danish for talking to a doctor

It is useful to name body parts, understand appointment vocabulary and explain what you want to say. This course focuses on language for healthcare situations and questions you may need to ask.

  • Practise body vocabulary and common expressions.
  • Recognise appointment and contact terms.
  • Ask for a word or message to be explained.

How it works

  1. You get one word or one sentence at a time. The most important come first.
  2. Answer correctly and it takes longer before it comes back. Answer wrong and it comes back soon.
  3. Already know some Danish? Take a short level test and skip what you know.
