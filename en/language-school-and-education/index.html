Danish for lessons and language-school messages

At a language school, you learn the language while also understanding messages about the course itself. Practise the words teachers use for tasks and deadlines. Then work on questions about modules, exams and further education in Denmark.

  • Ask for help with a classroom instruction.
  • Report an absence and ask about homework.
  • Recognise module-test and education vocabulary.

How it works

  1. You get one word or one sentence at a time. The most important come first.
  2. Answer correctly and it takes longer before it comes back. Answer wrong and it comes back soon.
  3. Already know some Danish? Take a short level test and skip what you know.
