Language for driving lessons and the driving test

Car parts, road users, signs and instructions have their own vocabulary. Use this course alongside driving instruction and choose the topics you want to become more familiar with.

  • Name the car and its parts.
  • Practise directions and positions in short messages.
  • Recognise traffic vocabulary from lessons.

How it works

  1. You get one word or one sentence at a time. The most important come first.
  2. Answer correctly and it takes longer before it comes back. Answer wrong and it comes back soon.
  3. Already know some Danish? Take a short level test and skip what you know.
