Danish on the production line and in packing

Food production involves equipment, ingredients and short work instructions. Start with your own area, such as cutting or packing. Then connect those words to questions about quantities, quality and the next task.

  • Ask how a product should be cut or packed.
  • Recognise label and temperature-reading vocabulary.
  • Report equipment issues or a production fault.
