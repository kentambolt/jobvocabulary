Danish words for explaining what happened

Short, specific information is useful when speaking to an assistance service. Practise words for the location, the incident and the people involved. The course also covers language for contact with the police and assistance services in Denmark.

  • Explain where help should be sent.
  • Describe an incident and answer questions.
  • Recognise reporting and document vocabulary.

How it works

  1. You get one word or one sentence at a time. The most important come first.
  2. Answer correctly and it takes longer before it comes back. Answer wrong and it comes back soon.
