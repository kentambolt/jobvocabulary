Danish for residents and maintenance tasks

A resident might describe a cold radiator or a door that will not lock. First learn the names of building parts. Then practise questions about location and timing, and short explanations of what will happen next.

  • Ask when a fault was noticed.
  • Explain an arrangement for access or repairs.
