Danish for directions and changing transport

A journey often involves several small messages: where to wait, which platform to use and when you will arrive. Practise one topic at a time, then combine transport vocabulary with numbers, time and questions about directions.

  • Ask whether a bus goes towards your destination.
  • Understand delay and platform-change messages.
  • Explain an address to a taxi driver.

How it works

  1. You get one word or one sentence at a time. The most important come first.
  2. Answer correctly and it takes longer before it comes back. Answer wrong and it comes back soon.
  3. Already know some Danish? Take a short level test and skip what you know.

Some of the most important phrases

DanskEnglish
Hvor kan jeg købe en billet?Where can I buy a ticket?
Du skal checke ind, før du stiger på.You must check in before you get on.
Hvilket spor kører toget til Odense fra?Which track does the train to Odense leave from?
Toget er forsinket.The train is delayed.
Undskyld, hvordan kommer jeg til banegården?Excuse me, how do I get to the station?
Jeg skal til den her adresse.I need to go to this address.

Jeg skal bruge en billet til Aarhus.I need a ticket to Aarhus.
