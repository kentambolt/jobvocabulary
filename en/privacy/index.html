Privacy and contact

Statistics stay off until you give permission.

Your progress

Your settings, progress, saved words and personal lists are stored in your browser. You can export your word lists yourself. The app does not send your word lists or answers to our statistics service.

Optional statistics

With your permission, we measure visits, the fixed page categories home, catalogue and practice, first use and brief activity signals while the page is visible. We also count course starts, the first answer and ten answered tasks. We send no raw page URLs, query parameters, referrers, words, answers, personal lists, names or email addresses.

Our own statistics server in the EU forwards the IP address and limited browser information to our internal Umami to estimate visits and sessions using a rotating salt. This can produce technical, pseudonymous visit estimates and information about browser, operating system, device type and approximate geographical area. Stored statistics fields contain no raw IP addresses or raw user-agent strings. A random ID per event is only a delivery ID. The client stores no visitor ID. Figures are estimates, not verified unique people.

Systems events are kept for 30 days and aggregate course counts for 90 calendar days. Umami visit history does not yet have automatic expiry. Encrypted backups may retain deleted data for a further 14 days.

Optional statistics are processed on the basis of your consent. We use no statistics cookies. Only your statistics choice and its timestamp are stored in the browser for up to 180 days. You can decline or withdraw permission here without losing access to exercises. Opting out, Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control stop both visit statistics and course measurement. Activity from before your permission is not sent later.

The web app and Android app use the same web content and statistics controls. Traffic is reported together because we cannot yet reliably distinguish the platforms. Blocking and lack of consent mean figures do not cover all visits.

Read-aloud uses your browser’s speech function. Your browser or operating system may use an online service to generate the voice.

The site is served through GitHub Pages. The hosting provider receives ordinary connection information, including your IP address, when serving the site. GitHub
