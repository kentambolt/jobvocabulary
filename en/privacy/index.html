Privacy and contact

Your progress

Your settings, progress, saved words and personal lists are stored in your browser. You can export your word lists yourself. The app does not send your word lists or answers to our statistics service.

Simple usage counts

We count course starts, the first answer and ten answered tasks as daily totals. We store no visitor ID or information linking your visits together. These are event counts, not unique people.

Measurement uses no cookies or browser storage. We respect your browser’s Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control. Events are sent without the page address, referring page, words, answers or information about your lists.

Daily totals are received on our server in the EU and kept for 90 calendar days. Backups may retain older counts for a further 14 days. The IP address is used briefly to protect against abuse, but is not stored in the statistics or sent to Umami or Google Analytics.

Read-aloud uses your browser’s speech function. Your browser or operating system may use an online service to generate the voice.

The site is served through GitHub Pages. The hosting provider receives ordinary connection information, including your IP address, when serving the site. GitHub

Contact and data controller

JobVocabulary is operated by Munthe Systems ApS, the data controller. Contact us about the app or your information: contact@jobvocabulary.com

Email is handled through Zoho EU and forwarded to Gmail.
