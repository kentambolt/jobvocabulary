Some of the most important phrases

DanskEnglish
Er hunden chippet og registreret?Is the dog microchipped and registered?
Jeg vil gerne bestille en tid.I'd like to book an appointment.
I byen skal hunden være i snor eller under fuld kontrol.In towns, the dog must be on a lead or under full control.
Må man have hund i lejligheden?Are dogs allowed in the flat?
Hunde og katte skal have et EU-selskabsdyrspas for at rejse i EU.Dogs and cats need an EU pet passport to travel in the EU.
Hvor tit skal hunden luftes?How often does the dog need walking?
Må jeg klappe din hund?May I stroke your dog?
Hvor gammel er hvalpen?How old is the puppy?
Min hund er syg.My dog is ill.
I skoven skal hunden være i snor – undtagen i en hundeskov, hvis du har fuld kontrol over den.In the forest, the dog must be on a lead – except in a dog forest, if you have full control of it.
Jeg spørger udlejeren først.I'll ask the landlord first.
