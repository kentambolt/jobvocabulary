Danish for housing and public services

Housing, letters and contact with public offices can introduce unfamiliar vocabulary. This course helps you practise the language and formulate the questions you want to ask.

  • Learn housing and practical vocabulary.
  • Recognise words in messages and letters.
  • Ask where to direct a question.

How it works

  1. You get one word or one sentence at a time. The most important come first.
  2. Answer correctly and it takes longer before it comes back. Answer wrong and it comes back soon.
  3. Already know some Danish? Take a short level test and skip what you know.
