Danish for the lorry working day

A transport job can involve dispatcher messages, loading-bay questions and customer contact. Learn document and equipment names, then connect them to short sentences about quantities, location and timing. The regulatory vocabulary in this course uses Danish and EU contexts.

DanskEnglish

Du må normalt køre højst 9 timer om dagen.Normally you may drive no more than 9 hours a day.
