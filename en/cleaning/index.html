Danish for cleaning work

Cleaning work brings instructions about rooms, tools, materials and what to do next. Practise equipment names and short messages together so that the vocabulary connects to tasks during a shift.

  • Ask which room to start in.
  • Recognise tools and cleaning products.
  • Say when a task is finished or ask for an explanation.
