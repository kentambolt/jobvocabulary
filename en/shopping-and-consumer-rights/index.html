Danish for purchases, parcels and product questions

Practise a shopping situation from finding an item to paying and keeping the receipt. You can also learn messages about a parcel or a problem with a purchase. Combine price and quantity words with short, polite questions.

  • Ask for a size, price or another item.
  • Explain what is wrong with a purchase.
  • Understand delivery, exchange and withdrawal terms.

How it works

  1. You get one word or one sentence at a time. The most important come first.
  2. Answer correctly and it takes longer before it comes back. Answer wrong and it comes back soon.
