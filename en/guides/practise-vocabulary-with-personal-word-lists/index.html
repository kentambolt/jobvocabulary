Practise vocabulary with your own word lists

A course gives you a starting point. Personal lists let you collect vocabulary you actually meet and practise those words together, alongside the existing courses.

JobVocabulary · 20.11.2026

Start practising

Save words with the heart

Use the heart beside a word to save it as a favourite or add it to a list. Choose an existing list or create a new one. Open My words and lists from the menu to find what you have saved.

Give each list a practical purpose, such as equipment at work, your next driving lesson or phrases you want to say. You can also add your own words and translations.

Choose your task types

Understand shows a word with answer choices. Write it lets you spell a word using the displayed letters. Say it yourself practises the other direction and can use word-building tasks for phrases.

Choose one task type or combine several to alternate between them. Change the selection before starting or through Task types and review in the menu.

Keep reviewing familiar vocabulary

With review enabled, learned words return occasionally. Difficult or forgotten words are treated differently from words you keep answering correctly, allowing new learning and checks of familiar vocabulary in the same practice routine.

Progress and lists are stored in the browser on the device you use. The export option in My words and lists lets you keep a copy of your lists. The current app does not automatically synchronise progress between devices.
