Danish vocabulary for driving lessons

Learning to drive in another language involves understanding both the subject and the words used to explain it. This guide focuses on the language you can practise alongside your driving lessons.

JobVocabulary · 20.11.2026

Start practising

Divide the vocabulary into topics

Begin with the car and its parts, then move to road users, road types and signs. Sidespejl, fodgænger and vejkryds mean side mirror, pedestrian and junction. Grouping related words gives them a useful context.

Direction and position words also matter: højre, venstre, foran and bagved mean right, left, in front and behind. Practise them in short sentences as well as on their own.

Recognise short instructions

An instruction can name an action and a location. Drej til højre ved næste vej means Turn right at the next road. Notice which words tell you the action and which tell you the location.

Keep a list of unfamiliar expressions to discuss with your driving instructor. Practise the vocabulary between lessons and use pronunciation playback to hear words again.

Combine language practice with driving instruction

Vocabulary practice helps with the language. It is not a complete driving theory course and does not replace instruction or the applicable traffic rules.

Choose Driving test for road vocabulary and phrases. Everyday essentials adds number and time vocabulary. Driver and courier also includes words used in transport work.
