Understand a Danish job advert

You do not need to understand every word in a job advert to find the main information. Start with the role, location and working hours. Then look for what the employer expects and how to apply. Use the advert to choose the vocabulary you want to practise first.

JobVocabulary · 21.11.2026

Start practising

Find the role and practical details

Mark the job title, workplace and words about working hours. Et deltidsjob and et fuldtidsjob describe the amount of work. Et vikariat tells you something different: it is a temporary post covering for someone. Find the actual hours and times in the individual advert rather than guessing from the heading.

Read this invented example: Køkkenmedhjælper søges. Deltid. Aftenvagter. Write three questions: What is the task? How many hours are there? What time of day is the work? If the advert does not answer, ask: Hvor mange timer om ugen er det?

Separate tasks, requirements and preferences

Look for headings such as Dine opgaver, Vi forventer and Vi tilbyder. Tasks tell you what you will do. Requirements and preferences describe the person the employer is looking for. The offer describes the workplace or conditions. Keep these three parts separate in your notes.

Notice wording such as et krav, en fordel and gerne. It can indicate whether particular experience is necessary or preferred. Also learn erhvervserfaring and sprogkundskaber. Save unfamiliar job terms in your own list and ask the employer if an important requirement is unclear.

Find the deadline and choose the next step

En ansøgningsfrist is the deadline for applying. Also find the contact person, how to submit the application and any attached documents requested. En tiltrædelsesdato, by contrast, is the date when work starts. Practise these two words separately so you do not confuse them.

Now choose one real advert. Write the role, hours, deadline and one question in your own words. The practice below covers finding jobs, describing skills and asking about the work. Use questions such as Hvordan søger jeg? and Hvad går arbejdet ud på? when you need more information.
