Danish for accounts, bills and bank questions

A bank letter or bill may contain amounts, dates and unfamiliar terms. Practise the vocabulary alongside useful questions. You might ask about a fee, explain a payment or describe a transaction you do not recognise.

  • Ask when a bill must be paid.
  • Explain which payment you want to investigate.
  • Recognise words about cards, interest and budgets.

How it works

  1. You get one word or one sentence at a time. The most important come first.
  2. Answer correctly and it takes longer before it comes back. Answer wrong and it comes back soon.
