Situations you can practise

Practise professional Danish for patient conversations and ward teamwork. Ask about symptoms, explain an examination and hand over to a colleague. Language practice does not replace clinical instructions or professional authorisation.

Some of the most important phrases

DanskEnglish
Hvad kan jeg hjælpe dig med i dag?How can I help you today?
Jeg vil gerne undersøge dig.I'd like to examine you.
Jeg vil gerne forklare, hvad vi har fundet.I'd like to explain what we have found.
Siger du ja til behandlingen?Do you agree to the treatment?
Skal vi tage overleveringen nu?Shall we do the handover now?
Her er din medicin.Here is your medication.
Det skriver jeg i journalen.I'll write that in your record.
Hvornår begyndte det?When did it start?
Må jeg lytte på dit hjerte?May I listen to your heart?
Det er ikke farligt, men det skal behandles.It isn't dangerous, but it needs treatment.
Som udgangspunkt må vi ikke behandle dig uden dit samtykke.As a rule, we may not treat you without your consent.
