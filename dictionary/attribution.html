The separate common-word collection is adapted from Wiktionary and is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International. The authors are Wiktionary contributors, identified in each entry’s page history. The source links for individual entries are included in the collection’s sources field.

Data was extracted using kaikki.org and Wiktextract. Reference: Tatu Ylonen (2022), Wiktextract: Wiktionary as Machine-Readable Structured Data, LREC 2022.

Changes include selecting common Danish meanings, removing unsuitable or duplicate senses, normalising spelling, reviewing and correcting translations, adding Polish, Romanian, Lithuanian and Bulgarian translations, and exporting compact records with stable IDs. The adapted collection is distributed under the same CC BY-SA 4.0 licence.
